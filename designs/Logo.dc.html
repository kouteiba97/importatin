Maabar — the mark

Maabar (معبر) means crossing point. The mark is an M built from two arches — two passages meeting on one line. Monoline geometric: one stroke weight, round caps, no gradient, no fill, no depth.

Primary lockup

Latin by default. The Arabic wordmark is used only inside the Arabic interface.

Clear space and minimum size

Leave clear space of at least one arch height on every side. Minimum size for the mark alone is 16px; for the full lockup, 96px wide. Below that, use the mark alone.
